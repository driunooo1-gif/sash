I don't want to make excuses for my mistakes. I know I have things I need to learn and improve, and I'm willing to do that. I want to become a better person, not just by saying it, but by showing it through my actions.

You became someone very special to me, and I don't want you to ever think that I don't appreciate you.

I'm not asking you to forget everything or instantly make everything okay. I just want you to know that I'm sorry, that I care about you, and that my feelings for you are real.

I'm sorry, Ganda. I love you. ❤️
